What we don't publish, and why

No uptime percentage appears anywhere on this page, and won't once Pro ships either. An honest uptime number requires a request path the number is actually measuring — and by design, Undercut mostly isn't in that path. A client that can't reach us falls back to its last verified cached policy, and if it has none, to the free static models.md tier→model map shipped in the skill itself, offline, no network required (see “What happens on a failed or expired verification” in the policy file). Since routing keeps working — just on slightly staler information — a percentage here would describe our servers, not whether coding agents kept dispatching correctly. We'd rather show three honest pending rows than one dishonest healthy one.
